# The Glärnisch from Zürich: snow and ice, 2017–2026

Interactive viewer of a citizen observation series: phone photos of the north-west face of the Glärnisch massif (Glarus Alps, Switzerland), taken from Zürich about 50 km away, georeferenced on swissALTI3D and checked against Sentinel-2 L2A.

**Open the viewer:** https://el1goluj.github.io/glarnisch-viewer/

Main 2026 result: the upper NW rim of the Glärnischfirn (SGI A50k-04; the ~10 ha patch OpenStreetMap labels "Bächistockfirn") ended summer 2026 with 1.1 ha of Sentinel-2 snow/ice signature (NDSI > 0.4), down from 2.7 ha in 2025 and about 11 ha in 2021. SGI2023 (2022 imagery) still maps 9.8 ha there.

Method, definitions and caveats are in the viewer ("Method, definitions and caveats"). The analysis was done with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic).
